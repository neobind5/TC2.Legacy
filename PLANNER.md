# Project Direction

## Gamemode Balancing (30%)

    - 1 to 3 maps per server, possibly adding smaller maps with resources around larger ones?
    (Reasoning: Cram players into tight spaces to speed up the game and stir conflict.)

    - Matches last 1-2 days each, this may increase as progression gains more depth.
    (Reasoning: Players should be expected to be able to reach endgame and fight it out before wipes.)


## Progression Rework (80%) - Actively working on!

    - Less pregenerated lootboxes. (Done!)

    - Make land a commodity through structures that passively generate resources. (Done!)

    - Add new uses for materials during progression,
    add more alloys and complex factorio-esque crafts.

    - Add new tiers before steel to ease progression,
    reducing setup time. (Done!)

    - Adding lategame content after steel and with smirglum,
    and pushing smirglum back in progression.
    (Note: Explosives will be neutered a lot to give mithril it's place.)
    (Reasoning: Endgame is currently very stale.)

    - Automatic refinement and resource production that grows overtime.
    (Note: Automated furnaces/autocrafters should be considered.)
    (Note: Remove personnel entirely? Static craft times?)

    - Shops sell absolute essentials, no skipping stages via money. (Done!)
    (Note: Better shops that can be created further in can be an option.)


## Simplifying Item Management (Done!)

    - Reducing the amount of interchangable items --
    (scraps, metal sheet/ingot/rod/frame) through removal of most bloat. (Done!)

    - Recipes that take predictable item quantities
    and produce predictable outputs with no garbage. (Done!)


## Building Rework (90%)

    - Buildings have very specific purposes,
    and only have recipes pertaining to that purpose. (Done!)
    (Reasoning: Reduces sensory overload when dealing with GUI/)

    - Smaller buildings. (Done!)
    (Note: Custom sprites + smaller hitboxes/)

    - More types of decor, more placeable decor.
    (Reasoning: Better looking bases. Low priority.)

    - All buildings and masonry made significantly cheaper. Tiered structural materials. (Done!)
    (Note: Remove foundation cost on most buildings)


## Faction Rework (50%)

    - Factions start from tents (Done!)

    - Neutrals can spawn at taverns, giving them a way to congregate together (Done!)
    (Note: Free to spawn, has dorm capacity and is a weak building.)

    - Neutral buildings are open to all neutrals, but not to factioned players. (Needs Testing.)
    (Reasoning: Bring back neutral coalitions.)

    - Factions can have their outposts stolen, converting nearby buildings to the new faction.
    (Reasoning: Makes total annihilation less of a priority during wars.)

## Vehicle Rework (25%)

    - Vehicles can be moved between maps through some method.
    (Note: Allowing humans to carry vehicles seems the easiest method ATM.)
    (Note: Ignore this if sticking with 1 map.)

    - Less arbitrary attachment flags, allow any attachment almost anywhere to increase possibilities.

    - Add wires that can transmit control between compatible objects.
    (Comment: This is to allow for much more interesting vehicle designs and keybind changing.)
